# Talaria 1.14.11 (versionCode 28)

## Fixed: the wrong part of a reply showed
- When Hermes replied to you and then called a tool (say, saving to memory) and added a closing line, only that closing line showed. The actual reply was hidden in "Worked for".
- Everything Hermes writes to you in a turn now shows in the reply bubble, in order. Tool calls and thinking stay folded in "Worked for". Copy and regenerate cover the full reply.

## New
- More > For nerds > **Full reply** (on by default). Turn it off to get the old fold-everything-but-the-last-message behaviour.

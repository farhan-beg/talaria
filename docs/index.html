Features

Not a webview. Not a wrapper.

A native Kotlin app built on the Hermes gateway protocol, so the things you do every day feel at home on a phone.

Chat that shows its work

Replies stream in as they're written. Tool calls and reasoning fold neatly away, approvals are answered on the card, and links open with one tap.

New

Todos & goals

A live checklist above the composer, and goal, loop and heartbeat controls you can pause.

Branch, undo, checkpoints

Branch a chat, undo the last turn, redirect a running reply, or diff and restore a checkpoint.

Every server, live

One connection per saved server. Switching servers leaves the other chats running, and each notification opens the right one.

Voice, both ways

Dictate your turns, and hear replies in your phone's voice or your server's TTS.

Automations

Cron jobs and scheduled prompts. Run them in the background and get a notification when they finish.

Private by default

Tokens are encrypted on the device. Nothing leaves for anywhere except your own server.

Get started

FAQ

Good questions.

What do I need to use Talaria?

A running Hermes Agent server with its dashboard reachable from your phone, and an Android phone on 8.0 or newer.

Is it official?

No. Talaria is an independent, community-built client and isn't affiliated with Nous Research.

Does it collect any data?

None. There's no analytics and no crash reporting. The app only talks to the servers you add, and tokens are stored encrypted on your device.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

For now, releases ship as signed APKs on GitHub, so updates land the moment they're built. Android asks once for permission to install from your browser.

Can I use more than one server?

Yes. Each saved server keeps its own live connection, so a chat on one keeps running while you work on another.

Found a bug or want a feature?

Open an issue on GitHub. Most issues get fixed in the next release.
